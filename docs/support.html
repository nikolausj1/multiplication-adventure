Multiplication Adventure · Last updated August 4, 2026

Multiplication Adventure is a times-tables game for iPad and iPhone. It turns the facts from 0 to 11 into a journey across seven worlds, each ending in a boss fight, with short daily quests, stars, streaks, a times-table reference chart, and a printable Certificate of Mastery at the end. It is free, works entirely offline, and contains no ads, no in-app purchases, and no tracking of any kind.

Something broken, stuck, or confusing? Email claude@justinnikolaus.com with the device you are using (for example, iPad 10th generation), the iOS or iPadOS version, and what happened just before the problem. A screenshot helps a lot. Because the app collects no data at all, nothing is reported automatically, so a note from you is the only way an issue can be found and fixed.

Frequently asked questions

How do I reset a player's progress?

Open the Parent Area by tapping the gear icon on the world map, pass the parent gate, then go to Players and tap Reset progress next to the player you want to reset. That returns the profile to brand new while keeping the name and avatar. Start over, just below it, also clears the name, avatar, and grade, and brings back the first-time setup. Both actions ask for confirmation first and cannot be undone.

How do I add another player?

Parent Area (gear icon on the map) → Players → Add. Type a name and the new profile is created with its own avatar. Each profile keeps its own stars, mastered facts, and certificate, so several people can share one device without affecting each other. Use Switch to change who is playing.

What is the parent gate, and how do I get past it?

Anything that changes or deletes progress sits behind a simple gate: the app asks you to solve a two-digit multiplication problem (for example, 16 × 18) — an adult-level task well beyond the tables the app teaches. Solve it and the Parent Area unlocks for that visit. A new problem is generated each time, there is no password to remember, and nothing about the answer is stored or sent anywhere.

How do I turn the answer timer on or off?

Parent Area → Settings → Show timer during practice. Turning it off keeps practice pressure-free; answer times are still tracked quietly so the app can tell fluent recall from slow recall. The optional Speed Round always shows its own timer, since that is the point of it. Sound effects can be turned off in the same place.

Does the app collect any data?

No. Nothing is collected, transmitted, or shared. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no way to enter an email address or any other contact detail. Progress, player names, and avatars are saved only on the device, and deleting the app removes them. Full details are in the privacy policy.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. The app works entirely offline, start to finish. It never connects to the internet, so it plays exactly the same on a plane or in the car as it does at home.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

No. There is no advertising, no in-app purchase, no subscription, and no paid unlock. The whole app, all seven worlds and all 77 facts, is included for free.

What ages is it for?

It is rated 4+ and suits anyone who is learning the times tables or coming back to them. The fact set is the standard 0 to 11 curriculum, sessions are short, and reading is kept to simple instructions, so a new learner and someone brushing up can both use it comfortably.

How does the practice actually work?

Each session is a short quest rather than an endless drill. New facts arrive a table at a time, easier tables first. Facts already known appear less often; facts still being learned come back more often, first as multiple choice, then fill-in-the-blank, then from memory. A wrong answer costs nothing, it just means the fact returns sooner. Three quest stars open a world's boss fight.

How do I see what has been mastered?

The Parent Area shows a per-fact breakdown of what is mastered and what still needs work, alongside overall progress. The Times Table button at the top of the map opens a reference chart that anyone can check at any time.

Where did the app go? It disappeared from my device.

Reinstalling from the App Store restores the app, but progress is stored only on the device and is deleted along with the app, so a reinstall starts fresh. There is no cloud backup, because the app has no server and sends nothing anywhere.
