Sound effects: original to this app.

Every sfx_*.wav in this folder is synthesized from scratch by
scripts/make-sfx.py in this repository. No samples, no asset packs, no
third-party audio, no external service, and no licence to honour. Running that
script reproduces these exact files.

Replaced 2026-08-24. The previous set drew on third-party CC0 packs (Kenney.nl
RPG Audio, artisticdude's RPG Sound Pack and WobbleBoxx's SoundPack01 via
OpenGameArt). Those files are public domain and were used legitimately, but they
are byte identical to the same packs in many other App Store apps, which made
them a plausible cause of the Guideline 4.3(a) "similar binary" rejection of
build 6 on 2026-08-13.
